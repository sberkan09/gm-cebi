**Full description** (max 4000):

```
GM Cebi ("GM's pocket") is a Turkish-first companion for 5E compatible tabletop roleplaying games. Look up a rule, a spell or a monster at the table, roll dice and keep track of initiative. No internet, no account, no data collection.

SEARCH
• One search box, Turkish or English: "kör" (blinded), "ateş topu" (fireball), "saving throw", "goblin"…
• Finds Turkish words even when typed without Turkish letters (cuce → Cüce).
• Rules, conditions, spells, monsters, classes, subclasses, species, backgrounds, feats and equipment: 1,088 entries.
• The search box sits at the bottom of the screen for one-handed use.

READ
• Spell cards, monster stat blocks, rule and condition pages.
• Switch between Turkish and English with a single TR/EN tap.

ROLL
• Expressions like 2d20kh1+5, 4d6dl1, (1d8+3)*2 and d%.
• Big dice buttons, ±1, advantage/disadvantage and roll history.

GM PACK
• Initiative tracker: order and rounds, hit points, damage/healing, condition tags. Add a monster straight from its page. The encounter survives closing the app.
• Session notes: autosaved, pinnable, searchable.
• The GM pack is unlocked in this version.

TRANSLATION STATUS
All of SRD 5.2.1 is in Turkish: 339 spells, 330 monsters, 258 magic items, 12 classes and their subclasses, origins, feats, equipment, conditions, the rules glossary and the gameplay rules (poisons, traps, encounter difficulty…). The translation is machine-assisted and still being reviewed; report any odd terms and we will fix them. Every page has a TR/EN switch to check the English original.

PRIVACY
The app never connects to the internet. No ads, no analytics, no account. Your notes and encounters stay on your phone.

LICENSE
GM Cebi is 5E compatible.

This work includes material from the System Reference Document 5.2.1 (“SRD 5.2.1”) by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. The SRD 5.2.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

The Turkish texts are a translation of the SRD 5.2.1 and a modified work derived from the original text.
```
